OXCINOB — Adsterra integration
================================
This package includes all 10 Adsterra ad-unit codes supplied by the site owner:
- 6 banner formats: 728x90, 300x250, 468x60, 160x300, 160x600, 320x50
- Native Banner
- Smartlink (user-click sponsored link; no automatic redirect)
- Social Bar script
- Popunder script

The Social Bar and Popunder scripts are loaded site-wide on the HTML pages.
Banner and Native placements are distributed across the home and main content pages.
Smartlink is presented as a clearly-labelled sponsored link.

Important:
- Ad networks may take time to approve/serve ads after deployment.
- Do not click your own ads or generate artificial traffic.
- Check Adsterra's current publisher rules and frequency controls for Popunder/Social Bar.
- If an ad unit does not serve, verify the code and domain status in the Adsterra dashboard.
