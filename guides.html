GUIDES

PLAY SMARTER.

Original, practical guides for improving your game without the hype.

SKILLS

Improve aim in mobile shooters

Start with a comfortable control layout. Practice the same movement patterns repeatedly, change one variable at a time, and judge settings by consistency rather than a single good match.

  • Keep your layout stable while practicing.
  • Make small sensitivity changes.
  • Practice tracking before speed.
  • Review what went wrong after matches.
SETTINGS

How to choose sensitivity

There is no universal perfect sensitivity. Use a training area and test small changes. If you overshoot targets, lower the setting; if turning feels slow, increase it gradually.

  • Change only one setting at a time.
  • Test short and long movements.
  • Keep notes so you can compare.
PERFORMANCE

Reduce lag and distractions

Keep your device updated, close unnecessary background apps, use a stable connection and avoid playing while the device is overheating.

  • Check free storage.
  • Use a reliable connection.
  • Lower graphics if performance drops.
ROUTINE

Build a 15-minute practice routine

A short routine is easier to maintain. Warm up movement, practice aim, then finish with a few focused matches where you apply one skill.

  • 5 min movement.
  • 5 min aim.
  • 5 min focused gameplay.
