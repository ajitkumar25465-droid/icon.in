UPDATE GUIDE

How to read patch notes

Check official notes for gameplay changes, balance adjustments, bug fixes, performance changes and event dates. Rumors can be useful for discussion, but verify important claims before sharing them.

CHECKLIST

Before a major update

Check storage, connection stability, official maintenance windows and release notes. Back up important local data where appropriate.

EXPLAINER

Why updates change the meta

Balance changes can alter which weapons, characters or strategies are effective. Treat a new meta as something to test rather than a guaranteed formula.
